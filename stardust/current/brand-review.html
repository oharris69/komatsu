The hero headline is barely larger than section titles

T-yellow-dormantagent-added

Declared brand yellow is almost unused

CSS ships a full secondary ramp around #ffc82f, but the homepage computes it on one 10x10px control; yellow appears only in photography. Direct will need to decide whether the target activates it or keeps blue-only chrome.

Source: _brand-extraction.json § cssUtilityRamps.secondary, palette notes
T-font-forkagent-added

A second family on news tags

News topic tags are set in Roboto 9pt while every other element is Noto Sans; both families load from Google Fonts. Direct will need to keep one family or name Roboto as a deliberate label face.
